## 2. Data We Collect

BIOME Pop is designed to work **without collecting personal data**. There is no sign-up or account of our own, **no ads** and **no analytics**: the game does not send usage data to our servers.

### 2.1 What stays on your device

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Game progress | Levels won, stars, high scores, gems, powers, lives, discovered creatures and settings. Saved **only on your device**. |
| Purchase record | The order ID of each in-game purchase, kept on the device only so the same purchase is never delivered twice. |

### 2.2 What goes through Google services

| Category | Description | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| In-app purchases | Gem packs and the Starter Pack are paid through **Google Play**. We never receive or store card or payment data; we only receive the order confirmation. | Google Play |
| Google Play Games | If you are signed in to Google Play Games: **cloud saving** of your progress (stored in your Google account), **achievements** and **leaderboards**. Leaderboards show your Play Games name and avatar, according to your Play Games privacy settings. | Google |
| Android backup | Android itself may back up the game data to your Google account's Drive, according to your device's backup settings. | Google |
| Crash reports | Android and Google Play may send technical crash and error reports (device model, OS version), according to your device settings. | Google |

### 2.3 Data we do NOT collect

- Real name, email, phone number, address
- Advertising ID (the game has no ads)
- Location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera
- Sensitive data (health, religion, biometrics, political opinion)
- Content from other apps

## 3. Purposes of Processing

1. **Running the game** — saving progress on the device and, if you use Google Play Games, in the cloud, so you can restore it on another device
2. **Processing purchases** — delivering the virtual items you bought
3. **Achievements and leaderboards** — showing your performance in Google Play Games
4. **Legal compliance** — responding to authorities and preventing fraud
