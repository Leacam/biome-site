## 2. Data We Collect

BIOME is designed to collect **the minimum personal data possible**. We do not request name, email, phone, address, photo, documents, or any sensitive personal data.

### 2.1 Automatically collected data

| Category | Description | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous player identifier | ID generated by Unity Authentication (anonymous sign-in). Not linked to a personal account. | Unity Gaming Services |
| Game progress | Biome state, discovered creatures, current era, momentum, seeds, settings. | App |
| Usage and analytics data | Gameplay events (merges, discoveries, sessions started/ended, duration), anonymous and aggregated. | Unity Analytics, Firebase Analytics |
| Technical device data | Model, OS, version, language, time zone, advertising identifier (if authorized by OS), IP address (anonymized by providers). | Unity, Firebase, Google AdMob |
| In-app purchases | Purchase confirmations (we do NOT store card data; all transactions go through Apple/Google platforms). | Apple App Store / Google Play |
| Advertising data | Advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID), ad personalization, fraud prevention. | Unity Ads, Google AdMob |

### 2.2 Data we do NOT collect

- Real name, email, phone, address
- Precise GPS location
- Contacts, photos, microphone, camera
- Sensitive data (health, religion, biometrics, political views)
- Content from other applications

## 3. Purposes of Processing

We process your data to:

1. **Run the game** — save progress, sync between devices (Cloud Save), restore sessions
2. **Improve the game** — understand which mechanics work, fix bugs, balance difficulty
3. **Local notifications** — send retention reminders (24h, 48h, 72h, 7 days) **scheduled locally on your device**, with no server tracking when or whether the notification was shown
4. **Display advertising** — app monetization, including personalization where you consent
5. **Process purchases** — complete transactions for virtual items
6. **Comply with legal obligations** — respond to lawful requests, security, fraud prevention

## 10. Security

We adopt reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your data, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all server communication
- Encrypted local saves
- Anonymous authentication — no password to leak
- Certified providers (Unity, Google, Apple) with SOC 2, ISO 27001

No internet transmission method is 100% secure. In case of a security incident affecting personal data, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority (ANPD/DPA) and affected users in compliance with LGPD art. 48 and GDPR art. 33–34.
