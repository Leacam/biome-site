## 2. How to delete your account inside the app

Deletion is performed from within the app, in a few taps:

1. Open the **BIOME** game.
2. Tap the **Inventory** icon (backpack) on the bottom HUD.
3. Tap the **Config** button.
4. Scroll to the end of the settings list.
5. Tap **"Delete my account"**.
6. Confirm in the warning dialog.

Deletion is **immediate**: the game reloads with a fresh account and no traces of the previous one.

## 3. Data deleted immediately

| Category | Where it lived | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous player identifier (Unity Authentication) | Unity Gaming Services | Deleted |
| Cloud save (progress, creatures, discoveries, Arca, Biodex, seeds) | Unity Cloud Save | Deleted |
| Entries in weekly and historical leaderboards | Unity Leaderboards | Deleted |
| Link with Google Play Games | Unity Gaming Services | Unlinked |
| Push notification token | Firebase Cloud Messaging | Deleted |
| Local save on the device | User's device | Deleted |

---

## 4. Data NOT automatically deleted

Some data, by nature or by operational requirements of third-party providers, is **not automatically deleted** when you delete your account:

- **Aggregated, anonymous events** already sent to Firebase Analytics and Unity Analytics — these follow the providers' retention policies (up to 14 months on Firebase by default). After deletion, these events no longer carry your account identifier and cannot be reassociated with you.
- **Purchase history** for in-app purchases — managed by Google Play Billing and under Google's responsibility.

To request deletion of these residual data, send an e-mail to **leirson.camargo@gmail.com** with your request. We respond within **30 days**, in accordance with article 18 of the LGPD.

---

## 5. Retention periods

- Unity Gaming Services account, Cloud Save, leaderboards and FCM token: **immediate** deletion upon confirmation in the app.
- Anonymous events in Firebase Analytics: up to **14 months** (default retention, configurable in Firebase).
- Events in Unity Analytics: as per the platform's configured retention.
